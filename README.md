# About

Shen Xu is an independent researcher whose work focuses on **AI Visibility, Agentic Search Optimization (ASO), AI-mediated systems, and the evolving decision structures of AI-native environments.**

His research explores how artificial intelligence increasingly mediates information discovery, recommendation, attribution, interaction, and decision-making across digital ecosystems. His work bridges practical digital marketing with broader questions surrounding AI governance, visibility, and economic coordination.

---

# Research Theme

> **Researching how AI transforms visibility, decision-making, and economic coordination across AI-native environments.**

My research examines the evolution of digital influence—from information distribution and platform ecosystems to AI-mediated selection, decision-making, and economic coordination.

Rather than viewing AI merely as a tool for retrieving information, I study how AI increasingly becomes an active participant in evaluating, recommending, selecting, and shaping decisions across digital society.

# Publications

## Books

### Decision Authority Economy

**Decision Authority Economy: How AI Reshapes Judgement, Systems, and Economic Power**

Introduces the concept of the **Decision Authority Economy (DAE)** and examines how economic value increasingly shifts from attention capture toward participation in AI-mediated decision pathways. The book explores how AI systems influence judgment, recommendation, attribution, and economic coordination.

**Amazon**

https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GY65JZ3H

---

### Agentic Search Optimization

**Agentic Search Optimization: A Practical Introduction to AI Search, Visibility, and Selection**

Introduces **Agentic Search Optimization (ASO)** as a framework for understanding how organizations, brands, products, and knowledge become discoverable and selectable as AI agents increasingly participate in information retrieval, recommendation, and decision-making.

Rather than treating AI search as an extension of SEO, the book argues that optimization is gradually shifting toward AI-native selection systems.

**Amazon**

https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H2Y7MYNR

---

### AI Visibility Marketing

**AI Visibility Marketing: Generative Engine Optimization, Agentic Search Optimization, and the Future of Digital Influence**

Explores **AI Visibility** as an emerging discipline and examines how Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), Agentic Search Optimization (ASO), structured knowledge, authority signals, and AI-mediated discovery reshape digital influence in AI-native environments.

The book connects practical marketing strategy with the broader evolution of AI-native information ecosystems.

**Amazon**

https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H4XBSYQD

# Research Vision

Artificial intelligence is becoming an increasingly active participant in how information is discovered, evaluated, recommended, and acted upon.

My long-term research seeks to understand this transition—from an Internet primarily organized around human attention to one increasingly structured by AI-mediated visibility, agentic selection, and decision infrastructures.

By combining practical industry experience with conceptual research, I aim to develop frameworks that help researchers, organizations, and decision-makers better understand how AI is reshaping digital society.
